FAQ

Questions we get before the first call.

How much does a website cost?

Most marketing sites land between $2,000 and $10,000 depending on page count, custom design and integrations. E-commerce and product work usually starts higher. We send a fixed quote after the first call — no hourly surprises.

How long does a project take?

A focused marketing site takes 4–6 weeks from kickoff. Larger builds with custom design systems or e-commerce run 8–12 weeks. We share a week-by-week plan before anyone signs anything.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes — roughly two-thirds of our work is international, mostly the UAE, UK and Canada. We adjust call times to your zone and everything runs through shared docs and a project channel.

What happens after launch?

You get documentation, a walkthrough recording and 30 days of post-launch support included. Many clients continue on a monthly retainer for improvements, content and SEO.
