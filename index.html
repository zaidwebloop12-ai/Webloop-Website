02 / 04
Why us

Why people stay.

Three years in, most of our work arrives through repeat clients and referrals. When we ask why, these are roughly the answers we get back.

Meet the eight of us

  • No templates. Ever.

    Your site gets drawn for your business, not bought and recoloured. It takes longer. It's also the entire point.

  • Fast, and we show you the numbers

    A page that takes four seconds to load has already lost half its visitors. We test on a mid-range Android over mobile data, not a laptop on office wi-fi.

  • You talk to whoever is building it

    No account manager relaying messages badly. The designer and the developer are both in the thread, from kickoff to launch.

  • Built to be handed over

    Clean code, a CMS your team can actually use, and written documentation. You should never be held hostage by your own website.

  • We're still here after launch

    Thirty days of support come with every build. After that, a monthly retainer if you want us to keep improving it, or nothing at all if you don't.

04 / 04 How it goes

Four stages, no silences.

You'll always know what's happening this week and what lands next. If something slips, you hear it from us before you notice it yourself.

  1. Stage 01

    Discover

    A long call, a lot of questions, and a written brief we both sign off before anyone opens a design tool.

    • Kickoff workshop
    • Audit of what you have
    • Scope, price, dates
  2. Stage 02

    Design

    Wireframes first, then the real thing. You see it in the browser, on your own phone, not as a flat picture in a deck.

    • Wireframes & flows
    • Visual design
    • Clickable prototype
  3. Stage 03

    Build

    Where most of the time goes. Weekly demos on a staging link so nothing arrives as a surprise at the end.

    • Responsive build
    • CMS & integrations
    • Weekly staging demo
  4. Stage 04

    Launch

    We test on real devices, fix what breaks, then ship. A walkthrough recording lands in your inbox the same day.

    • QA & accessibility pass
    • Deploy & analytics
    • Handover & 30 days support
