About us

We're Webloop Agency — A Team Building the Future of Digital.

Founded in Karachi in 2023, we're a compact studio of designers, developers and strategists who care more about what a website does than how it looks in a portfolio shot — although we care about that too.

Work with us See our work
Who we are

A small team with an unfair amount of care.

Webloop started with two people, one desk and a stubborn belief that most business websites were being built backwards — design first, purpose later. Three years on we are a team of eight, and the belief hasn't changed.

We work with founders, marketing leads and product teams across Pakistan, the Gulf, the UK and Canada. Some come to us for a single landing page. Others hand over the whole digital presence — brand, site, store and the marketing that feeds it.

What stays constant is how we work: small senior teams, direct communication, and no project so big that you cannot get the person building it on a call the same day.

“They asked harder questions in the first week than our last agency asked in six months. The site that came out of it doubled our qualified leads.”

Sara Iqbal — Head of Marketing, Nova Finance
Karachi, PakistanWorking with clients in 6 countries

Our mission

Make great digital work accessible to ambitious businesses.

Premium design and engineering should not be reserved for companies with in-house teams and seven-figure budgets. We build at agency quality with studio-sized overheads, so more businesses can afford work that actually performs.

Our vision

Become the digital partner brands in the region grow with.

Not a vendor you hire once, but the team that ships version one, then version four — knowing the business well enough to spot the opportunity before it lands in a brief.

Our values

Four rules we don't bend.

Values are only real if they cost you something. These have cost us projects — and kept the ones worth keeping.

Craft over shortcuts

We would rather ship one considered detail than ten rushed ones. Quality is the only marketing we rely on.

Radical clarity

Plain language, honest timelines and no invented jargon. You always know what we are building and why.

Results, not decoration

Beautiful work that does not move a metric is a hobby. We design against goals and measure what happens after launch.

Partnership mindset

We work as an extension of your team — sharing context, pushing back when it helps, and owning outcomes together.

Our story

Three years, fifty launches.

  1. 2023

    Two people, one desk

    Webloop started as a two-person studio in Karachi taking on small business websites and never missing a deadline.

  2. 2024

    First product work

    We moved beyond marketing sites into product UX and shipped our first end-to-end design system for a SaaS client.

  3. 2025

    Going international

    Clients in the UAE, UK and Canada joined the roster, and the team grew to eight designers, developers and strategists.

  4. 2026

    50 projects live

    Fifty projects shipped, a 98% satisfaction score, and a retainer practice keeping those launches improving month after month.
